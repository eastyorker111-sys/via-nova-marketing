Review what needs to be kept.

No retention period has been decided. This tool finds records for an owner review; it never deletes records or sets a deletion deadline.

Review each case

  1. Check the original request, recent communication, owner notes and follow-up date. Database activity includes saved owner edits and messages, not unseen email or WhatsApp conversations.
  2. Check the project/invoice workbook, mailbox, contracts and any open support or dispute. A closed stage or an old date is not permission to delete.
  3. In the private inbox owner notes, record the review date, purpose for retaining it, evidence or unresolved question, and next review date. Set the follow-up date. Preserve useful existing notes.
  4. For an access or correction request, verify the requester’s identity and authority first. Export only the selected case for internal review; remove internal notes and third-party information before any separately authorized disclosure.

Case export includes this enquiry, its owner fields, communication records, notification state and audit metadata. It does not collect external email, historical Formspree records, project files, invoices or provider backups. It is not a ready-to-send customer data response.

There is no deletion button, automatic purge or new schedule. An owner decision and separately authorized procedure are required before deleting anything, including backup copies.
