A manageable working rhythm.

Each working day

  1. Refresh private enquiries; review genuine records.
  2. Record stage, next action and follow-up date.
  3. Check agreed project work and client reviews.
  4. Use verified channels for personally approved replies.
  5. Save and close the canonical workbook.

Each week

Review enquiry quality, decided outcomes, actual delivery time, workload and unpaid invoices. Check native search/social reports with dated context. Choose one useful improvement.

Each month

Reconcile payments, refunds, direct costs and renewals. Review access and necessary retention. Back up private records and test a safe restore.

Cost boundaries

No new paid plan was activated for this ecosystem. Existing domain, hosting, email and creative-tool bills still need reconciliation. Free provider quotas are shared and limited. Do not infer a zero monthly business cost.

Business model and commercial gates

Serve owner-led Canadian businesses through planning, branding/materials, websites/ecommerce and content/campaign support. Project scope and price are agreed before work; ongoing support is separately agreed. Actual prices, delivery capacity and paid demand require evidence. Estimate hours, direct supplier/licence costs, overhead, revisions and contingency before committing.

Recover a failed submission or update

Keep the form open and retry unchanged with its reference. Check the private store before creating another request. A stale owner update must be refreshed and reconciled; never overwrite blindly. An email alert is not storage proof.

Privacy and access

Keep original requests separate from owner notes. Collect only what is needed; an enquiry is not newsletter consent. Customer records belong in protected systems, never URL tracking, public reports or this local launcher. The local HTML file is not an access-control system.
