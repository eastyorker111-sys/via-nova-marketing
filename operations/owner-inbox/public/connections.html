Business hub · Enquiries and follow-up

From website enquiry to client work.

One Marketing enquiry store. Owner decisions stay separate from customer answers. No customer message is sent from this guide.

1. A visitor tells you what they need

Homepage quick contact, situation cards, education cards, services, the Contact page and the detailed project brief all use the same enquiry endpoint. Guided answers arrive inside the original message. Direct phone, email and WhatsApp links open their own channels; they do not create website records.

2. The website saves the enquiry

The same-site /api/enquiries route forwards through the private server connection to the Marketing Worker. It validates the request and saves it to the dedicated Marketing D1 database, together with its notification record. A matching reference is returned only after storage is confirmed.

Each request receives an enquiry reference and a separate customer reference. Repeating the same submission does not create a second record. Customers are not merged by name, phone or email.

3. You review it in the private inbox

Open enquiries and follow-up. Search by reference, business or request. Move the stage from New to Discussing, Quoted and then the actual outcome. Record the next action and follow-up date. Use the date filter each working day.

Email alerts are off. Check the private inbox for website enquiries; direct emails arrive at vianovamarketing@outlook.com. No mailbox import runs automatically.

4. Keep the conversation with the enquiry

Open its communication timeline. Record relevant email, phone, meeting or WhatsApp notes with the exact reference. Sending still happens through your approved email or messaging app. Recording a draft does not send it. Automated customer reply import is not connected.

ViaNovaConnect is shared with other businesses. Confirm Marketing and the customer reference before recording anything here. Never identify the business from the phone number alone.

5. Hand over agreed work

Save the owner fields, then use Copy project handover. Add the project to the existing private Projects workbook with the same enquiry and customer references. Confirm scope, price, acceptance and capacity before committing work.

Projects, invoices, payment evidence and calendar bookings are currently owner-managed. They do not synchronise automatically. A Won enquiry is not proof of payment. Record the actual invoice and transaction references and book the dedicated Marketing calendar only when ready.

Connection status

  • Website → dedicated database → private inbox: live, verified with labelled tests on 4 November 2026.
  • Owner stage, notes and follow-up: saved to the same record with revision protection.
  • Email alerts: off. Website storage and private inbox remain active.
  • Outlook reply import: not connected. A dedicated Outlook connection is still required.
  • WhatsApp automatic sync: not connected; shared receiver ownership and phone check remain deferred.
  • Projects, invoices and calendar: existing manual workflow, no new payment or booking automation.

If something fails

A visitor should keep the form open and retry unchanged. In the CRM, keep unsaved notes and resolve a stale-update warning before trying again. Never treat an alert as storage proof. Export private backups through the inbox; restore only into an isolated review store.
